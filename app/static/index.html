About YatraSetu AI

A minor project (B.Tech CSE, AI/ML) at Roorkee Institute of Technology. Setu means bridge: it connects pilgrims with verified safety information in their own language.

Assistant pipeline

From question to grounded answer

🎙️InputText or voice (Web Speech API)
🌐LanguageScript detection, Bhashini for other languages
🎯IntentTF-IDF + logistic regression. Emergencies are routed to a fixed safety reply.
📚RetrieveHybrid TF-IDF over the bilingual knowledge base, re-ranked by shrine
📡Live dataCrowd forecast and route risk
🧠AnswerOpenRouter LLM via LangChain, or extractive. Numbers checked, PII redacted.
🔊ReplyText with sources, read aloud on request

🎙️ Voice: current limits

  • Voice input uses the browser's Web Speech API. It works in Chrome, Edge and Safari, not in Firefox.
  • It needs microphone permission and localhost or HTTPS.
  • Chrome sends audio to its own cloud speech service, so it does not work offline.
  • English (en-IN) and Hindi (hi-IN) only. Garhwali, Kumaoni and other languages are planned through Bhashini speech recognition.
  • Read-aloud uses the voices installed on the device. Some devices have no Hindi voice.
